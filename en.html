About Us

Built by people who've worked
on both sides of the Pacific

Creative Arts Tokyo was founded by a team who have worked across the entertainment industries of both Japan and New York. We license Broadway productions and bring together an international group of directors, cast, and crew for every show.

Every performance is in English — the real thing, not a translated adaptation. For Japanese-speaking guests, we project Japanese subtitles on screen, so nothing gets lost while the energy of a real Broadway-style show stays intact. Audience participation is part of the design: this isn't a show you just watch, it's one you take part in.

Backstage / Sponsorship

Support Us From Backstage: Equipment & Sponsorship

We're a small company, but we care deeply about sound and staging that feels like the real thing. If you can help with equipment or sponsorship, we'd love to have you involved from behind the scenes.

Equipment We're Currently Looking For

Wireless Mic Wireless microphones for dialogue and singing. The more units we have, the more of our cast can be heard singing live.
Mic Belt / Pack Belts and pouches that secure transmitter packs to performers. These wear out quickly given how dance-heavy our shows are.
Projector For projecting Japanese subtitles on screen. Brightness and resolution needs vary by venue, so having a few options on hand really helps.
Stage Lighting Portable spotlights and LED bars that bring presence even to small venues — ideally something we can carry to outreach performances.
